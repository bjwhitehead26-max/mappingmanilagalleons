**WHAT IS THE BENSON?**

The Nettie Lee Benson Collection is one of the premier repositories for historical documents, maps, images, books, and videos related to much of Latin America. Specifically, the archive holds over 970,000 books, 19,000 maps, 93,500 photographs, 4,000 feet of manuscripts, 11,500 broadsides, and other 50,000 items. Named after famous librarian, Nettie Lee Benson, who managed the collection from 1942 to 1975. She was responsible for increasing the collection's size tenfold during her tenure constantly traveling to various nations acquiring new collections for the archive.

**PACIFIC SOURCES** 
The Benson contains dozens or more (so I've found thus far) sources related to the Spanish Pacific. These are primarily in the forms of maps, commercial letters, and other administrative sources listed in the guides compiled by famous librarian, Carlos E. Castañeda in the 1930s. This source likeley had been housed in Mexican archives up until that point. Probably in the _Archivo General de la Nación_ (AGN) which is where I have found other Pacific-related documents.
