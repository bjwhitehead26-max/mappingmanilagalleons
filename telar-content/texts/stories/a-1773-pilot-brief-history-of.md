---
title: "Brief History of the Manila-Acapulco Galleon Trade"
---

**1565:** Conquistador Miguel López de Legazpi led an expedition from Navidad in New Spain to Cebu in the Philippines. Augustianian friar and navigator, Andrés de Urdaneta, discovers the return route to Mexico. Reaches Acapulco.
**1570:** Legazpi leads the conquest of Manila. Takes it from the Muslim leaders who ruled over the region and establishes the port as a Spanish trade hub. The galleons anually sail unobstructed from this port until the Seven Years War.
**1762:** British forces led by Coronel William Draper temporarily capture the port, blocking Spanish trade.
**1813:** Acapulco is captured by revolutionary José Mari̇a Morelos during the Mexican War of Independence (1810-1821). 
**1815:** The last Manila galleon departs Acapulco for Manila.
