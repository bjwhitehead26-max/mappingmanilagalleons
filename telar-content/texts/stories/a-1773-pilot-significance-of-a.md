title: "Significance of a Pilot Diary?"
---

Much of the content of these sources are merely navigational terminology, cardinal directions, distance measurments, dephs, or other important topics regarding the operation of the vessels at sea. However, the pilots of the Manila-Acapulco galleons (as well as all Spanish ships) occasionally wrote down other interesting or horryfying experinces at sea from sickness to religious rituals. By carefully reading these sources, they offer insights into daily life on the five-to-eight month long trans-Pacific voyages. Their detailed measurments also serve as useful points for creating geo-spatial representations of the voyages, which is my primary mode for researching, comparing, and experimenting with this topic.
