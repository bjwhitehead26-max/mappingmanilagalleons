As I continue to fomulate this final project, I want to ask a series of questions when combining information from differnt sources:
1. In what ways did cosmographical understanding for pilots differ in their studies in Spain versus on the ground (sea) in the Pacific?
2. What was the esperince level of each pilot? Did everyone have experince or training in Spain? How many pilots were experinced navigators from other empires?
3. How did climatic shifts following the Little Ice Age of the seventeenth century affect Pacific navigation? How did seasonal weather patterns change the durations or routes of voyages if they departed later or sooner than normal?
4. Why did pilots choose to write down certain qualitative experinces and not others?
5. How does the way that the derroteros describe Pacific navigation differ with what the diarios reveal?
6. What do these sources also reveal about the early-modern marine ecosystems? In what ways are mentions of sea life and vegetation represent academic curiosities or strategic intentions on part of the pilots?
7. What do the rare mentions of the rest of the crew reveal about the culture on the ships?
8. What tools were available to pilots in the early modern Pacific and how did they evolve over the centuries?
