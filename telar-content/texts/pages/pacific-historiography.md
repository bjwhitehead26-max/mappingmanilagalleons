Pilot Navigation Diaries (
For my research, I am deeply fascinated in what one seemingly quantitative source reveals about life on the early-modern Pacific Ocean. Pilot navigational diaries are overwhelmingly underutilized by trans-Pacific historians. The reasons for this vary, but most scholars have opted to study global aspects related to the Manila-Acapulco galleon trade often focusing on economic and cultural exchange between East Asia, New Spain, and Peru.
